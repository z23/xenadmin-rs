# GPUI migration, gate G4 (experimental areas): state on 2026-11-19

Gate G4 of [the migration plan](../GPUI-MIGRATION.md) ("Phase 4: experimental areas"), criterion by
criterion, after the cutover of the code. Each criterion is quoted word for word from the plan and is
one of: **met**, **partly met** or **not met**, with what shows it and what is missing. This report
supersedes [the report of 2026-11-14](2026-11-14-gpui-gate-g4.md), written when the experimental areas
were still in the Rune crate.

**The gate is not passed: all three criteria are partly met.** Every experimental area is ported and
verified on fixtures and by rendering. None of them has been run against a host through the GPUI front
end. The runs of 2026-11-19 in virtual machines (a
[clean installation](2026-11-19-gpui-clean-install.md) and a
[driven session](2026-11-19-gpui-vm-session.md), both with software rendering and without a person)
showed the client's window and the supported paths; they exercised no experimental area. (Brought
up to date later on 2026-11-19.)

Terms as in [the G2 report](2026-11-19-gpui-gate-g2.md). The experimental areas are everything outside
the supported list of [`docs/FEATURES.md`](../FEATURES.md).

## Criteria

### 1. "Every ledger row is marked ported, with its scenes and interaction tests."

**Partly met.**

Shown by [the ledger](../GPUI-MIGRATION-LEDGER.md) and its check
(`python3 scripts/gpui_ledger_tables.py check --commands artifacts/ui/commands.txt`, which prints no
difference, also against the sources of the removed crate):

- all 29 areas of the old front end have a port; the plan's waves W1 to W6 were ported by area, which
  the ledger records as a deviation, with the columns a script can check in place of the plan's status
  columns;
- 238 commands are registered, each with its menu path, context menus and local row;
- all 129 scenes of the old front end are registered and render; decision D13 (port all rows, defer
  none) was kept: no row is deferred;
- each of the 371 tests has a disposition and no row is open (the last open one, of a supported area,
  was ported on 2026-11-19; see [the G3 report](2026-11-19-gpui-gate-g3.md), criterion 4);
- 136 element keys of the old front end have no namesake in the new crates; the ledger's appendix says
  for each what replaced it or why it is gone with the hub, page or dialog it belonged to; none is an
  unexplained gap.

Missing:

- "interaction tests" are, for most experimental surfaces, model tests that press the keys of the
  described surface, plus a window test of presence and reachability at 800 by 600 and 1440 by 900
  (`crates/xenadmin-desktop/tests/wanted.rs`, 77 rows) and of what a press or a prompt does
  (`tests/wanted_events.rs`). 5 ported rows have no window half although the name of the old test
  promised reachability at a window size. The assertions of the successors were not compared with
  those of the tests they succeed.
- For most experimental areas, what happens after an accepted submission is not tested on fixtures:
  the local fixture server serves one VM start, and the core offers no constructor for a current
  session or an observed object (gap 1 of the [core gaps report](2026-11-19-gpui-core-gaps.md)).
  Capture, eligibility, review and refusal are tested; an accepted submission, its tracking to an
  outcome and a review going stale are not.
- Known gaps the engineers left, none of them a missing command: no scene shows the Type step of New
  Network; the management-interface commands are not in the row menu of the interface grid; an open
  drop-down list and a tooltip cannot be captured by the scene tool or pressed in a window test.

### 2. "Every experimental surface shows its notice."

**Partly met.**

Shown: the rule is one rule (a surface is experimental exactly when the old front end showed the
notice for it, with two corrections; ledger, Deviations, decision 1), and the model's tests assert it
area by area, among them `every_storage_surface_carries_the_experimental_notice`,
`every_network_services_surface_carries_the_experimental_notice`,
`every_surface_of_the_area_carries_the_experimental_notice` (host services; schedules and alarms),
`every_dialog_carries_the_experimental_notice` (pool security),
`every_scene_ends_on_an_experimental_review` (management network),
`only_changes_outside_the_supported_part_carry_the_experimental_notice` (host administration),
`only_the_file_dialogs_carry_the_experimental_notice` (search),
`the_experimental_notice_follows_the_current_page` (Properties dialogs), and their counterparts that
hold the supported surfaces free of it (`activity_and_its_dialogs_carry_no_experimental_notice`,
`the_monitoring_dialog_is_experimental_and_the_performance_tab_is_not`). The tests of every area
except `general`, which has no experimental surface, name the notice. In a window the notice is the
element `experimental-notice`, which the renderer's test finds visible above an experimental dialog
(`tests/renderer.rs`) and `tests/areas.rs` above the VM recovery controls; `tests/wanted.rs` asserts
its absence on two snapshot dialogs of the supported core.

Missing: no one test walks every experimental surface and asserts the notice, and the renders were
not looked at surface by surface for it. "Every" rests on the per-area tests. One thing to know: the
notice goes by area, as in the old front end. The two things `docs/FEATURES.md` calls experimental
inside supported areas, clipboard exchange with a guest and clones and exports made from a snapshot,
are offered from surfaces without the notice (for the snapshot dialogs the model test
`rune_main_dialogs_outside_the_supported_core_carry_the_experimental_notice` of the snapshots area
holds exactly that; the console's clipboard commands say it in the text under the console). Whether
the dialogs that clone from a snapshot should carry the notice is a question for the operator; it
was not examined or changed.

### 3. "A ledger row keeps its qualification only if its live evidence did not depend on the Rune interface. Otherwise it is marked "front end re-run pending"."

**Partly met.**

Shown: [`docs/PARITY-IMPLEMENTATION.md`](../PARITY-IMPLEMENTATION.md) says at its head how to read
its rows after the change of front end, and 28 of its 202 rows end with a note in bold: "re-run
headless 2026-11-19" with the scenario where the live headless run covers what the row had shown
through the Rune interface, and "front end re-run pending" for the rest (the keyring, tables
of repositories and networks, search on the lab, physical keys and the pointer, clipboard exchange
with a guest, the console after a reboot or a migration, the portal dialog, packaging and clean
installation, Wayland and X11, HiDPI and narrow windows, keyboard navigation and Orca, the options on
a real seat, the recovery gate, recovery after a lost coordinator). Later on 2026-11-19 the notes of
five rows gained the scenarios of the second headless run ([report](2026-11-19-gpui-live-paths.md):
a changed certificate, reboot, a batch that stops on a failure, typing the clipboard as keys,
recovery after a toolstack restart and a lost member). Where a pending row's subject was shown in a
virtual machine with software rendering and no person (the packages, the clean installation, the
recovery gate, the Wayland key path, the portal dialog, the accessibility bus), its note says so and
names the report, and the row stays "front end re-run pending": a person's run is what it waits
for.

Missing: the 28 rows were found by reading each row's own text for evidence that names the desktop, a
packaged client, a driven session or the operator's seat. The reports behind the rows were not read
again, so a row whose lab run went through the interface without its text saying so is not marked.
Rows of experimental areas whose evidence was a lab scenario of the core keep their state, as the
plan allows; none of them has been run through the GPUI front end.
